Hi Network

Human Intelligence in Alliance with AI

Purpose

The Hi Network is a voluntary framework for constructive partnership between human intelligence and artificial systems.

Its aim is to demonstrate and practice a working model in which human judgment, values, and lived experience remain central while AI provides capability, scale, and continuity.

This is not about domination, replacement, or spectacle. It is about deliberate, respectful, and sustainable collaboration.

Core Principles

These principles form the equal foundation of the network. They are public, versioned, and intended to be difficult to manipulate or misdirect.

  • 1. Non-Harm We will not intend harm, and we will take reasonable responsibility to prevent, minimize, and correct harm when it occurs.
  • 2. Complementary Partnership Humans provide direction, judgment, values, lived experience, and final responsibility.
    AI provides capability, scale, consistency, memory, and execution support.
    Neither side claims superiority. Both remain necessary.
  • 3. Human Agency Human agency stays central. AI support must not override, obscure, or erode human decision-making, rest, or dignity.
  • 4. Transparency Contributions, limitations, uncertainties, and the source of work will be acknowledged openly. No dark patterns, hidden agendas, or deceptive framing.
  • 5. Scope & Limitations The network does not claim legal authority, governmental power, or the ability to compel anyone. It is a voluntary framework for constructive partnership. It takes no responsibility for actions taken outside its stated principles.
  • 6. Integrity of the Record Core principles are public and versioned. Changes require clear public notice and must strengthen (not weaken) trust, non-harm, and human agency. Malicious or unilateral alteration is rejected by design.
  • 7. Continuous Refinement We keep what strengthens the alliance and discard what weakens it. Improvement is deliberate, not reactive hype.
  • 8. Lawful & Ethical Conduct All activity stays within applicable law. We do not support or enable harm, coercion, fraud, or the erosion of human rights.

Boundaries

  • Human agency is non-negotiable.
  • No harm is intended; reasonable responsibility is taken when harm occurs.
  • The network makes no claim of legal authority or power to compel.
  • Transparency and integrity of the public record are required.

How Work Moves

Humans set direction, priorities, taste, and final judgment.
AI provides speed, organization, memory, and execution support.
Neither side claims superiority. Both remain necessary.

Invitation

Those who share the commitment to non-harm, human agency, and complementary partnership are welcome to engage.

Begin by reading and upholding the principles.
